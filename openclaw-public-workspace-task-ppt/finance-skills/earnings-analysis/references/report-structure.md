# Report Structure

## Page 1 — Summary
- Headline conclusion: beat/miss/in-line and thesis impact.
- Rating and price target if coverage context exists.
- 3-5 key takeaways.
- Table 1: headline metrics vs consensus/prior guidance.

## Pages 2-3 — Detailed Results
- Revenue, EPS, margins, cash flow.
- Segment/geography drivers.
- Why results differed from expectations.

## Pages 4-5 — Key Metrics & Guidance
- Operating KPIs and management guidance.
- Compare current guidance with prior guidance and street expectations.

## Pages 6-7 — Thesis Update
- What improved, deteriorated, or remains unchanged.
- Catalysts and risks after the print.

## Pages 8-10 — Valuation & Estimates
- Estimate revisions table.
- Valuation snapshot and sensitivity if useful.

## Appendix — Sources
- Earnings release, filing, transcript, presentation, consensus source, and prior guidance materials.
