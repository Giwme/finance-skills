# Best Practices

## Good Headlines
- "Revenue Beat Driven by Segment X; Margin Guidance Raises FY EPS by 4%"
- "Top-Line Miss Offset by Better Cost Control; Thesis Intact but Catalysts Delayed"

## Avoid
- Relying on model training data for recent earnings.
- Using earnings-call transcripts with mismatched dates.
- Plain URLs instead of clickable hyperlinks in DOCX deliverables.
- Full initiation-style company background.

## Quality Checklist
- Every figure/table has source and date.
- Consensus source/date is cited for beat/miss.
- Current and prior guidance sources are both cited.
- SEC filings link to EDGAR when available.
- Estimates table shows old vs new.
- Conclusion clearly states thesis impact.
- Analysis includes the disclaimer: not investment advice.
